| 2026-09-24 | AN-1 | Whole-request results: network, 5xx, 408, 429, unexpected JSON, and `ACCOUNT_NOT_FOUND` → retry with backoff. `UNAUTHENTICATED` and other 4xx → stop until the next trigger (app start, sign-in, or a new change). | 2026-09-24 | AN-1 | Results are applied only to rows the user hasn't changed since they were sent. A newer change is sent in the next pass of the same run (up to 3 passes). | Never overwrite an edit or delete made while a request was in flight. |
